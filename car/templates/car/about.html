About the showroom

A premium Django case study for luxury automotive portfolios.

The project stays fully inside Django, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Bootstrap while offering a much richer product experience.

Why it works

Refactored for scale and real client demos

The application now supports brand normalization, unlimited gallery images, account-based customer actions, live AJAX catalog filtering, comparison tools, and protected booking flows.

The design direction keeps the original black and gold identity, but lifts it with glass panels, motion, depth, and conversion-focused card interactions suitable for portfolio and freelancing work.

Operational highlights
  • Normalized inventory models for brand and gallery scale
  • Built-in authentication with protected wishlist and bookings
  • Database-backed contact leads and admin approval workflow
  • Deployment-friendly settings with environment variables and secure defaults
